🎧 CX Quick Reference

Crate & Barrel Call Support — quick phrases & workflows

Acknowledge → Impact → Ownership → Action
Rule: Don't stack empathy statements. Make one specific acknowledgement, then move into action.

Delivery Delay

I can certainly understand how frustrating it is to be expecting your order and have the delivery date change. Let me take a look at the order and see what options we have.

Damaged Item

I'm sorry your item arrived damaged, especially after waiting for it to arrive. Let's get this taken care of for you.

Frustrated Customer

I can hear how frustrating this situation has been, and I appreciate you bringing it to our attention. Let me review the details and see what I can do from here.

Previous Contact

I'm sorry you've had to spend additional time reaching out about this. Let me take a closer look so I can provide you with the most accurate information.

Event Coming Up

I can certainly see how important it is to have this in time for your event. Let me review the order and see what we can do to help.

Unavailable Item

I understand you were expecting to receive this item, so I can appreciate the disappointment of finding out it's currently unavailable. Let me check what options we have.

Long Wait

I understand you've been waiting quite some time for your order, and I appreciate your patience. Let me check the latest status and see what options are available.

Cancellation

I understand you no longer need the order. Let me review the order details and see what options are available for you.

Call Opening

Thank you for calling Crate & Barrel. My name is [Name]. May I have your name, please?
It's a pleasure to assist you, [Customer Name]. How may I help you today?

After Customer Explains

Absolutely, [Name]. I can help with that. Let me pull up the order and take a look at the details for you.

Delivery

Delivery Delay

I can certainly understand how frustrating the delay is. Let me check the latest delivery information and see what options are available.

Delivery Window

I'm seeing an estimated delivery window of 2–4 PM. Please keep in mind that this is an estimated window and may be subject to change.

Furniture / In-Home

Since this is a furniture order and the address is within the applicable delivery area, let me review the available in-home delivery options.

Long Distance

Let me review the order details and delivery location to confirm whether this qualifies as a long-distance delivery and what options are available.

Order Handling

New Order

I'd be happy to help you place an order. May I have the SKU number for the item you'd like to purchase?

Cancellation — Shipped

Since the order has already shipped, I'm unable to cancel it at this stage. Once it is delivered, we can review the available return options for you.

Replacement

I'd be happy to help with a replacement. Before I proceed, I'll need 3–5 photos showing the damage from different angles.

Backordered

The item is currently out of stock, but the order is in fulfillment and can move forward once inventory becomes available.

Returns & Refunds

Return Request

I'd be happy to review the return options for you. Let me first check the order and item eligibility.

Refund

Once the return is processed according to the applicable policy, the refund will be issued back to the original form of payment.

Restocking

Depending on the item and return eligibility, a restocking fee may apply. Let me verify the specific details for your order.

CCS Quick Terms

COI — Customer Order Insurance. Use the current CCS definition/policy when explaining coverage.

SRF — Customer Contact Support. Use the current CCS workflow/options shown for the customer contact.

SKU — Product/item identifier used to locate the item.

LOC / LONG / STD — Delivery/order selections. Always follow the current CCS workflow and account guidance.

Training note: CCS labels and policies can change. Follow the current information displayed in your work system rather than relying on this page for policy decisions.

Recap

Just to make sure I have everything correct, you're looking for [solution], and the order is [key detail]. Is that correct?
To recap, I've [action taken], and the current estimated delivery date is [date].

Closing

Is there anything else I can assist you with today?
It was a pleasure assisting you today. Thank you for choosing Crate & Barrel, and have a great day!
